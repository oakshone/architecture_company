Identify Problems

Red: indicates there is a serious, potentially dangerous issues - you should stop the car when it's safe and get it checked by a professional mechanic. Yellow/Orange: something is wrong that needs to be checked but it's not as urgent as a red light - take extra care and get it looked at when you can.

Speedometer problems are usually caused by electrical or mechanical issues, such as a faulty speed sensor, bad wiring, a blown fuse, a problem with the instrument cluster or the vehicle's ECU, or a broken physical cable in older vehicles.

Common tire problems include under-inflation, over-inflation, uneven tread wear, cracks, bulges, and punctures. These issues can lead to poor handling, reduced fuel efficiency, and potential blowouts.

When your car starts showing signs of trouble—such as unusual noises, dashboard warning lights, or performance issues—the first step is to contact a trusted mechanic. Start by identifying a reputable auto repair shop, either through personal recommendations, online reviews, or local directories.
